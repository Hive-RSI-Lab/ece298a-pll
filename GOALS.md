# ece298a-pll goals

This repository is the term project of ECE 298A (Integrated Circuit Design and Tapeout) at the University of Waterloo: a charge-pump PLL for one Tiny Tapeout tile on GF180, with a divide-by-8 prescaler and a programmable feedback divider. Its analog half (the bias, the charge pump, the loop filter and the ring VCO) lives in `blocks/pll/analog`, its digital half (the phase-frequency detector, the dividers, the lock detector and the control registers) in `blocks/pll/digital`, and the two meet in `blocks/pll/interface.yaml`. `bash tests/check.sh` is the fast check every pull request has to pass. This is coursework, so runs never submit anything to a course system.

### Close digital issue 8: the digital half meets timing with the VCO clock at the post-layout VCO ceiling plus margin

- **Status:** open; ss tried by design on 2026-10-30 (task 261030-162622, #41), closed to within 0.03 ns but without margin. vco_out is timed per process at the extracted VCO's ceiling plus margin (`harden/constraints.sdc`; `analog/reports/recorded/pex_pvt.json` vco_f_vdd_mhz, vctrl at the rail): ss 3.75 ns (267 MHz) against 263.3 MHz (ss_pff, which is ss, 125 C, -10% supply, the STA corner's own), tt and ff 2.7 ns against 290.7 and 299.5 MHz. At ss the first /2 stage missed by 0.11-0.15 ns, all of it the flow's generic 0.25 ns clock uncertainty. What was tried, with numbers: (c) every library flop and inverter pair at ss in Liberty: only the reset-less dffnq_2 + inv_1 beats dffrnq_2 + inv_1, by 0.095 ns, and without a reset the gate-level sim holds the stage at X; a clock buffer before the flop gains nothing (Liberty barely moves with clock slew); the routed loop loses about 0.12 ns to wire, mostly the 16 um D-to-Q span inside the 20 um flop, and placing the three cells by hand (two layouts, each hardened) read -0.03 ns at ss max against the placer's own +0.004, so placement stays the placer's. (b) vco_out's only vco_out-to-vco_out path is that toggle loop: one clock pin, so no skew, and the ceiling already includes vctrl at the rail and the low supply, so its uncertainty is period jitter alone. The SDC sets it to 0.10 ns (setup, vco_out to vco_out only), derived next to it from the analog spec's jitter estimate (2.5 ps rms at ss, scaled x2.8 for the noise it leaves out, 7 sigma = 83 ps). Over three routed runs ss setup then reads -0.03..+0.004 ns at max_ss, -0.005..+0.022 at nom, +0.015..+0.038 at min (it was -0.11..-0.15), tt +0.79..+0.81, ff +1.57..+1.58, hold unchanged: which ss corners pass depends on how the run routes the D net. That is closure at best, not margin: the stage toggles at about 274 MHz routed, 4% above the ss ceiling, and the jitter allowance takes most of it. Real margin at ss needs (a), a TSPC /2 in the analog macro so the digital half clocks at vco/2 (analog layout, DRC, LVS and pex_pvt 31/31 re-run); no library cell gets there. A recovery forces DN after 1024 unlocked reference cycles, the backstop for a VCO above the stage.
- **What:** The first prescaler stage has to toggle at whatever the VCO reaches, or the feedback divider miscounts and the loop runs away to the rail. Time vco_out at what the extracted VCO can reach on each process, with margin, and meet it at every STA corner. This changes nothing under `blocks/pll/analog`.
- **Done when:** the digital timing gate passes with vco_out at each process's post-layout VCO ceiling plus margin (`tests/score.py` checks the SDC's periods cover the ceilings pex_pvt measures), setup and hold met at every STA corner.

### System verification: the loop locks on the extracted analog netlist at the corners that bound its range

- **Status:** open, the loop locks at all four corners and vctrl settles at two (task 261030-162628, issue #42). On the extracted netlist the loop locks at 100 MHz and holds at ff_pss (6.72 us), ff_m40c_vm10 (4.00 us), ff_125c_vp10 (4.48 us) and ss_m40c_vm10 (4.96 us), with f_vco within 4.1e-5. vctrl's p-p over the last 10 us is 20.9 mV at ff_pss and 27.4 mV at ff_m40c_vm10, against REQ-LOOP-VCTRL's 20. Between them vctrl is flat to about 1 mV: the excess is isolated pump correction kicks through R1, which the bench's zero-delay PFD and its 1 ns sync grid make (`blocks/pll/tb/README.md`). What's left is to find whether a finer sync or the gate-level PFD's reset pulse removes them, or whether they are the cell's, and to decide the bound with that.
- **What:** The course grades a system verification on Nov 26. The cosim gate locks the released RTL with the schematic netlist at tt_27c only. `blocks/pll/tb/cosim_corners.py` runs the same bench, at the same 100 MHz point and bounds, with the cell swapped for the layout's parasitic extraction and the PDK corner, temperature and supply moved to each corner that bounds the post-layout range. It picks those corners from the recorded post-layout sweep (`analog/reports/recorded/pex_pvt.json`): the lowest and highest VCO ceiling (ff_pss 126.4, ff_m40c_vm10 299.5 MHz), the lowest and highest phase margin (ff_125c_vp10 34.5, ss_m40c_vm10 52.9 degrees), and the lowest Kvco and pump current (ff_pss again). A corner takes about 25 minutes of wall time, more than the score's budget, so its result is recorded in `blocks/pll/reports/recorded/cosim_corners.json` with a hash of every input, and counts only while those hashes hold, as bench_strength's does.
- **Done when:** the recorded `cosim_corners.json` shows lock (lock time <= 50 us, no drop, f_fb and f_vco within 0.1 %, vctrl within 20 mV p-p over the last 10 us) at every corner `bounding_corners()` picks from the recorded pex_pvt, with its inputs unchanged (`tests/score.py`'s two "co-sim locks at 100 MHz on the extracted netlist" lines at 1).

# ece298a-pll goals

This repository is the term project of ECE 298A (Integrated Circuit Design and Tapeout) at the University of Waterloo: a charge-pump PLL for one Tiny Tapeout tile on GF180, with a divide-by-8 prescaler and a programmable feedback divider. Its analog half (the bias, the charge pump, the loop filter and the ring VCO) lives in `blocks/pll/analog`, its digital half (the phase-frequency detector, the dividers, the lock detector and the control registers) in `blocks/pll/digital`, and the two meet in `blocks/pll/interface.yaml`. `bash tests/check.sh` is the fast check every pull request has to pass. This is coursework, so runs never submit anything to a course system.

### Close digital issue 8: the digital half meets timing with the VCO clock at the post-layout VCO ceiling plus margin

- **Status:** open, done-line moved 2026-10-30 (task 261030-095411, #31). The old line, vco_out at about 2.7 ns at every STA corner, could not be met: no library flop toggles at 2.7 ns at ss, and the extracted VCO tops out at 115-274 MHz, never near 370. `harden/constraints.sdc` now times vco_out per process at the extracted VCO's ceiling on that process (`analog/reports/recorded/pex_pvt.json` vco_f_vdd_mhz, vctrl at the rail, every temperature, supply and resistor skew) plus margin: 4.0 ns (250 MHz) at ss against its 246.8 MHz ceiling (ss_pff), so with the 0.25 ns uncertainty the hand-placed dffrnq_2 + inv_1 first stage (about 3.65 ns routed) must toggle at 267 MHz, 8% above it; tt and ff keep 2.7 ns (370 MHz) against ceilings of 269.4 (tt_pff) and 274.2 MHz (ff_m40c_vm10). The VCO and the flops share a die, so an ss STA corner meets an ss VCO. The SDC picks the period from the liberty each STA corner links, which LibreLane's own signoff STA honours (it reads the SDC fresh per corner). The chip-flow timing gate does not yet: it reads `final/sdc`, OpenROAD's written-back copy with one literal period (2.7 ns), so ss still reads about -1.2 ns there until that gate evaluates the signoff SDC per corner (hive-chip-flow brief, queued). Before the change ss setup was -1.2 ns at 2.7, tt +0.65, ff +1.43; at 4.0 ns ss reads about +0.1 ns from the same numbers. A recovery forces DN after 1024 unlocked reference cycles, the backstop for a VCO above the stage. Revisit if the post-layout ss ceiling passes about 265 MHz.
- **What:** The first prescaler stage has to toggle at whatever the VCO reaches, or the feedback divider miscounts and the loop runs away to the rail. Time vco_out at what the extracted VCO can reach on each process, with margin, and meet it at every STA corner. This changes nothing under `blocks/pll/analog`.
- **Done when:** the digital timing gate passes with vco_out at each process's post-layout VCO ceiling plus margin (`tests/score.py` checks the SDC's periods cover the ceilings pex_pvt measures), setup and hold met at every STA corner.

### Test the whole PLL loop in co-simulation

- **Status:** done 2026-10-28. `python blocks/pll/tb/run_layers.py` runs the linear model, the RTL with a calibrated behavioural cell, and the RTL with the transistor-level analog netlist through cocotbext-ams. The last of these is the `cosim` gate, which passes: lock in 4.48 us at 6.25 MHz x 16, VCO at 100.000 MHz, with the lock detector held. `blocks/pll/tb/README.md` writes it up. Only the nominal point is gated.
- **What:** The course grades a system verification of the term project. The proposal (`blocks/pll/brief/proposal-classic-pll.md`) tests the loop as a whole in three layers: a linear model of the loop, the real digital RTL in cocotb with Python models of the pump, filter and VCO calibrated from the analog benches, and a mixed-signal co-simulation of the digital RTL with the analog netlist. Build those benches under `blocks/pll` and run them. This reads the analog half but does not change it, so it can go ahead while the sign-off above is pending.
- **Done when:** the three layers run from one command in this repository, the co-simulation shows the loop locking at the 100 MHz nominal point with the lock detector asserting, and the result is written up next to the benches.

### Lay out the analog half and match it after extraction

- **Status:** open (moved up 2026-10-28). `blocks/pll/analog/layout/gen_pll_analog.py` passes drc (0) and lvs (unique match). On 2026-10-29 the post-layout bench runs at every corner the spec scores: `layout/pex_pvt.py` runs `layout_ref/pll_analog_pex_pvt_tb.cir` (the pex bench with the corner left open, bounds = spec.yaml post_layout_bounds) through the engine's sim_run at all 31 corners on the extracted netlist. 29 of 31 pass. Two miss: at tt_27c the VCO reads 196 MHz at vctrl = vdd-0.3 V against the 200 MHz floor, and at ff_pss Kvco is 35 MHz/V against 40. Task 261029-152124 redrew the ring block (gate vias over the left pad, columns 0.6 um apart, NAND/output tops level with the stage inverters, channel pitch 0.8): r1..r5 carry 9-25% less extracted C and tt_27c reads 202.7 MHz, a pass. ff_pss stays at 36.1 MHz/V; with every ring node's wire C taken out it would read 44, so it needs a design decision (R_V2I or the bound). ss_m40c_vm10 failed after that: the bias reference's start-up never released there (vst held high, MN_ST's kick pulling vbp and, through MN_B2 in triode, vref down to 0.76 V, below MN_STD's threshold), leaving Ib at ~42 uA (Icp 168 uA, bias_ref 0.51 V). Pre-layout it released by 0.08 uA; extracted, not at all. Task 261030-001020 made MN_ST 4 um long (was 2): with vst forced high the cell now pulls it down by at least 15.9 uA at every corner, extracted (2.0 uA was the worst outside ss_m40c_vm10 before), the pre-layout start-up at ss_m40c_vm10 takes 0.92 us (was 1.63), and post-layout ss_m40c_vm10 reads Icp 17.6 uA: 30 of 31 corners pass, ff_pss Kvco the one miss. tt_pss passes or fails depending on magic's element order ("Dynamic gmin stepping failed" before true gmin stepping converges is read as an engine error). magic's extresist also counts each net's coupling C twice: once as coupling, once as ground C on its rnodes. The 226 MHz recorded at typical before was optimistic: magic's parasitic extraction dropped the coupling capacitance of every unlabelled net, about half the wire C on the ring nodes, so the generator now labels every routed internal net. Post-layout the VCO's ceiling (vctrl = vdd) is 110-266 MHz over the corners, below the pre-layout 150 MHz floor at ff_pss (110 MHz) and right at it at ss_125c_vp10 (150.0) and tt_125c_vp10 (151.0). Started from pll_en rising with no kick (`layout_ref/pll_analog_pex_start_tb.cir`), the extracted ring runs at all 31 corners with vctrl at vdd-0.3 V, but not with vctrl at 0.3 V at ff_125c or tt_125c (all three supplies each; tt_125c on c0301c6's extraction too). ss_m40c_vm10 starts since MN_ST was lengthened. H2 is presented as challenge H2-a9fad5 and waits for the owner.
- **What:** Once the analog sign-off is recorded with the owner's approval, draw the layout of the analog half, pass DRC and LVS against the signed-off netlist, extract its parasitics, and rerun the analog benches on the extracted netlist against the spec's post-layout bounds. Matching, the charge pump's mirrors and the VCO ring's symmetry are the places where layout can eat the thin margins the review lists.
- **Done when:** DRC and LVS pass on the analog layout, and every analog bench passes its post-layout bounds on the extracted netlist at every corner the spec scores.

- **Status:** open (moved up from Later 2026-10-29; work on it had already started). The first top_harden attempt (2026-10-28) was refused by the engine: an msde top with two clock domains gets no constraints.sdc, and the engine rebuilds `top/` on every run. - **What:** With the analog layout done, harden the digital half around the analog macro into one tile, run full-tile DRC and LVS, and pass the Tiny Tapeout precheck. Replace the template placeholder in `src/` and the template README with the real design, so the Tiny Tapeout gds, docs and test workflows build and test this design rather than the template.
- **Done when:** the tile hardens with no DRC or LVS errors, the Tiny Tapeout precheck passes, and the gds, docs and test workflows pass on main with the real design in `src/`.

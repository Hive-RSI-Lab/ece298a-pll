# ece298a-pll goals

This repository is the term project of ECE 298A (Integrated Circuit Design and Tapeout) at the University of Waterloo: a charge-pump PLL for one Tiny Tapeout tile on GF180, with a divide-by-8 prescaler and a programmable feedback divider. Its analog half (the bias, the charge pump, the loop filter and the ring VCO) lives in `blocks/pll/analog`, its digital half (the phase-frequency detector, the dividers, the lock detector and the control registers) in `blocks/pll/digital`, and the two meet in `blocks/pll/interface.yaml`. `bash tests/check.sh` is the fast check every pull request has to pass. This is coursework, so runs never submit anything to a course system.

### Close digital issue 8: the digital half meets timing with the VCO clock at the post-layout VCO ceiling plus margin

- **Status:** open, done-line moved 2026-10-30 (task 261030-095411, #31). The old line, vco_out at about 2.7 ns at every STA corner, could not be met: no library flop toggles at 2.7 ns at ss, and the extracted VCO tops out at 115-274 MHz, never near 370. `harden/constraints.sdc` now times vco_out per process at the extracted VCO's ceiling on that process (`analog/reports/recorded/pex_pvt.json` vco_f_vdd_mhz, vctrl at the rail, every temperature, supply and resistor skew) plus margin: 3.8 ns (263 MHz) at ss against its 262.3 MHz ceiling (ss_pff); tt and ff keep 2.7 ns (370 MHz) against ceilings of 293.1 (tt_pff) and 302.9 MHz (ff_m40c_vm10). Those are the settled ring's ceilings (task 261030-095422): the post-layout bench had read the top of the range 10..45 ns into the tran, with the bias still settling, 4-8% low (ss 246.8, tt 269.4, ff 274.2), and the ss period was 4.0 ns against that. At 3.8 ns, with the 0.25 ns uncertainty, the hand-placed dffrnq_2 + inv_1 first stage (about 3.65 ns routed, 274 MHz) would have to close in 3.55 ns, about 0.1 ns short; the stage is still 4% above the ss ceiling itself. The VCO and the flops share a die, so an ss STA corner meets an ss VCO. The SDC picks the period from the liberty each STA corner links, which LibreLane's own signoff STA honours (it reads the SDC fresh per corner). The chip-flow timing gate does not yet: it reads `final/sdc`, OpenROAD's written-back copy with one literal period (2.7 ns), so ss still reads about -1.2 ns there until that gate evaluates the signoff SDC per corner (hive-chip-flow brief, queued). Before the change ss setup was -1.2 ns at 2.7, tt +0.65, ff +1.43; at 3.8 ns ss reads about -0.1 ns from the same numbers. A recovery forces DN after 1024 unlocked reference cycles, the backstop for a VCO above the stage. Revisit if the post-layout ss ceiling passes about 265 MHz.
- **What:** The first prescaler stage has to toggle at whatever the VCO reaches, or the feedback divider miscounts and the loop runs away to the rail. Time vco_out at what the extracted VCO can reach on each process, with margin, and meet it at every STA corner. This changes nothing under `blocks/pll/analog`.
- **Done when:** the digital timing gate passes with vco_out at each process's post-layout VCO ceiling plus margin (`tests/score.py` checks the SDC's periods cover the ceilings pex_pvt measures), setup and hold met at every STA corner.

- **What:** The course grades a system verification of the term project. - **Done when:** the three layers run from one command in this repository, the co-simulation shows the loop locking at the 100 MHz nominal point with the lock detector asserting, and the result is written up next to the benches.
